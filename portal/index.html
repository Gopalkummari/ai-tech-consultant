The Economic Thesis

Coding costs have collapsed by 90% due to modern coding agents. Agencies can no longer sustain payroll charging high hourly rates for basic programming.

Consulting margins have shifted to 3 non-coding bottlenecks:

  • Domain Knowledge Retrieval (thousands of platform help guides & screenshot settings).
  • Support Velocity (drafting verified replies in the company's voice).
  • Discovery & Scoping (detecting $0 native settings vs expensive custom dev).

Mathematical & Algorithmic Foundations

1. Honest Abstention Gate

Confidence score $C \in [0, 1]$. If $C < 0.35$, the system refuses to guess, halts hallucination, and automatically routes the inquiry to the Human Expert Escalation Queue.

2. Diff Extraction Learning

Extracts token-level differences $\Delta = \text{HumanEdit} - \text{AIDraft}$, converting stylistic supervisor adjustments into persistent guidelines inside learned_rules.json.

3. Scope-Diff Risk Engine

Calculates set-theoretic deltas $\Delta = M_2 \setminus M_1$, isolating scope additions and dropped features to compute net billable hours and protect agency profit margins.
